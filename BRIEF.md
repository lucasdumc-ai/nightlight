# Project brief: night light

## The competition

- I’m entering the Presidential SI Challenge (Standard Track), a national K–12 competition. My age group: Elementary (grades K–5)
- Judges score only a written entry (a PDF of up to 10 pages) and a video of up to 4 minutes. They reward a clear understanding of the problem and a sensible use of AI.
- My deadlines: December 18, 2026, 5:00 pm Eastern. December 23, 2026, 5:00 pm Eastern

## The problem

- People in the world struggle with stopping to use their phones because people get bad bed time and they do bad at school.
- Impact area: Something else
- Why it matters (impact): they will have better bed times, spend more time with family, do better in school.
- Evidence it’s real: Sleep and phone use can be connected to school performance.

A study of 1,257 students ages 12–18 found that 71% experienced daytime sleepiness, and 41% had failing grades under the study’s definition. The researchers also found that greater use of mobile devices was connected with lower academic performance.

Website: PubMed Central, part of the U.S. National Institutes of Health

- What people told me: they don't know what time it is like: they watch and 2 hours passed with them thinking they just watched for a little bit.

## My solution

- My AI’s job: My AI helps by stopping the person from using their phones, which works better than setting a time limit because the person can ignore the limit and keep on using their phones.
- Tools that already exist, and how mine is different: Forest, Freedom. Mine won't let people ignore the time limit.
- Safety plan: wrong timer-clicking on the timer sends you to a real time page.
- Emergency pass not working—everything you click gives you an emergency pass.

## My MVP features (build these first, in this order)

1. The user can set a daily goal for minimal phone usage.
2. The user can create custom time blocks for focused work periods.
3. The user can choose specific apps to block during study time.
4. The AI gives correct, safe answers to real questions.
